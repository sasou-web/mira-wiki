    Mira wiki

    Everything to install Mira, connect it to your Jellyfin, watch at home and away, and with friends.

    On this page

    1. Pick your device
    2. What you need
    3. The short version
    4. Where to get help

    Mira is a free Jellyfin client for Windows, Mac and iPhone. Jellyfin keeps your films and series on a computer of yours and serves them; Mira is the pleasant way to watch them. No Mira account, no ads, and nothing leaves your server.

    Pick your device#

    DeviceWhat it needsGuide
    WindowsWindows 10 (2004 or later) or 11, x64Install on Windows
    MacApple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or laterInstall on Mac
    iPhone and iPadiOS 26 or later, AltStore Classic, a free Apple IDInstall on iPhone

    There is no Mira for Android for now. Mira web, which Jellyfin used to serve to phones in the browser, ended with 0.9.4.

    What you need#

    • A Jellyfin server, 10.9 or later, on a computer that stays on, and an account on it. No server yet? Mira for Windows installs Jellyfin and sets it up for you: see Set up Jellyfin.
    • Your films and series in Jellyfin's folders, named the way it recognises them.
    • To watch away from home: Tailscale, free. Mira has it built in, so most devices need nothing more. See Away from home.

    The short version#

    1. On the computer that will hold your videos, install Mira for Windows and choose No server yet: Mira installs Jellyfin on this PC.
    2. Put your videos in the Movies, Shows and Anime folders it creates.
    3. Open the guide (F1) and click Set everything up on the Mira on your iPhone card.
    4. Install Mira on your other devices and sign in with the same account.
    5. Send your party link to a friend and watch together.

    Where to get help#

    • Search this wiki with the field at the top of the page, or press /.
    • Troubleshooting lists the messages Mira shows and what to do.
    • Ask on Discord, or report a bug on GitHub.

    Note

    Mira is a preview: it is used every day, but some things have not been tried on every setup yet (HDR, multichannel audio, several monitors). Mira is an independent project, not affiliated with Jellyfin, and provides no films.
