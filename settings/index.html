    Windows and Mac#

    Settings open from the gear in the left rail (⌘ + , on Mac).

    PartWhat is in it
    PlaybackSkip intros and recaps, play episodes back to back, trailer before the film, hide spoilers, show missing episodes, "still watching?", resume where you left off, picture, quality away from home, hardware decoding, the mpv engine (Windows)
    Audio & subtitlesPreferred audio and subtitle languages, subtitle size and style, audio output and amplifier
    AppearanceMira's language, couch mode, home rows, library density, automatic carousel, reduce animations
    Account & serverYour Jellyfin account and photo, sign out, Built-in Tailscale, new episode notifications, who is watching on Jellyfin right now
    Mira on this PC / MacUpdates, and the titles kept to watch offline
    PrivacyEverything Mira sends, to whom and when, with the switch of each connection that has one
    Your recapWhat you watched this month and this year, from Jellyfin

    Under Your server, for Jellyfin's administrator:

    PartWhat is in it
    Friends & guestsAdd a friend (an account of their own), your party link and its guests, allow a device by its code
    MaintenanceTitles to fix, automatic subtitles, missing episodes, movie collections, seek bar previews, and on the Jellyfin PC: backup and update of Jellyfin, conversions with the graphics card

    The guide (? in the rail, F1 on Windows) is separate: where your videos go, and the Mira on your iPhone card that gets the server ready.

    iPhone and iPad#

    The Settings tab has:

    • Account: your server, your account, your photo, Sign out.
    • Playback: languages, intros and recaps, next episode, resume a little earlier, hide spoilers, show missing episodes, and Mobile data (quality cap on cellular, 2 Mb/s by default).
    • Away from home: Built-in Tailscale and the path Mira takes to Jellyfin.
    • Administration (Jellyfin administrator): who is watching on Jellyfin right now.
    • This iPhone → Privacy: everything Mira sends, to whom and when, and Built-in Tailscale's switch.
    • About: the version and the source code.

    Language#

    Mira speaks English and French. By default it follows the language you chose when installing it, otherwise your system's. Change it in Settings → Appearance → Mira's language: it applies the next time Mira opens. The default audio and subtitle languages follow it.

    Couch mode#

    For a PC or a Mac plugged into the TV, watched from the couch (Windows and Mac). Turn it on in Settings → Appearance → Couch mode: it applies at once, and stays on the next times Mira opens.

    • Mira fills the whole screen, and every page is drawn larger, as on a 1280 × 720 window: readable from across the room. The player keeps its size.
    • A white ring shows what is selected. The arrow keys go from one thing to the next anywhere on the screen, the rail included; Enter opens, Esc or Backspace goes back.
    • In the player, Esc or Backspace stops the film and goes back to its page, as on a TV.
    • A game controller (Xbox, or one that works as one; PlayStation and other controllers paired with a Mac): A opens, B goes back, Y searches, LB and RB change page. In the player, A pauses, X turns subtitles on or off, Y skips the intro, LB and RB jump a minute. Plug a controller in with couch mode off and Mira offers to turn it on.
    • A TV remote, through the TV's HDMI-CEC with an adapter that types keys: its arrows, OK and back work like the keyboard's, and its play and pause keys reach Mira like a keyboard's media keys.

    Note

    Couch mode reads the controller only while Mira is in front, and sends nothing anywhere.

    To leave it, Settings → Appearance → Couch mode again.
