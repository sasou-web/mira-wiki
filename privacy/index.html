    Privacy

    What Mira sends, to whom and when. No account, no analytics, no telemetry.

    • Windows
    • Mac
    • iPhone

    On this page

    1. Always
    2. Only when you use a feature
    3. On your devices
    4. Report a problem

    Mira has no account, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. It talks to your Jellyfin server, and to a few services only when a feature needs them, each listed below. The code is open: see for yourself.

    Tip

    The same list is in the apps: Settings → Privacy on Windows, Mac and iPhone, each connection with what it sends and its switch, where it has one (the update check, Built-in Tailscale, and on Windows the check for a newer Jellyfin).

    Always#

    WhoToWhat
    Mira, every deviceyour Jellyfin serveryour library, playback and progress, with your session's token in a header, never in an address (except the iPhone's player stream, which Apple's player cannot send otherwise)
    Mira for Windows and MacGitHub (api.github.com)the update check, at startup then every 6 hours: the request itself (your IP address, Mira's version), nothing else. Turn it off in Settings → Mira on this PC → Update automatically

    Before you sign in, Mira looks for Jellyfin on this computer, on your home network (Jellyfin's discovery question, never beyond your network) and on your Tailscale devices. Nothing identifying is sent; your password goes only to the address you sign in to.

    Only when you use a feature#

    FeatureContactsWhat
    Built-in Tailscale, once you sign in to itTailscale's coordination servers and relays; your router (UPnP, NAT-PMP, PCP), asked to open a port for a direct connection rather than through a relaylike the Tailscale app: the account you chose, the device's name, its system and IP addresses, its public key. Videos stay end-to-end encrypted. No logs sent
    The mpv engine (Windows)SourceForgethe download of the engine, SHA-256 checked
    Installing or updating Jellyfin (Windows)repo.jellyfin.org, GitHub (winget's catalogue, once a day on the Jellyfin PC, off in Settings → Privacy)the download, and whether a newer version exists
    Automatic subtitlesOpenSubtitles, SubDL, from your serverthe file's fingerprint and size, IMDb/TMDb ids, season, episode, title, year, language. Never the file's name
    Search for subtitles… with another Jellyfin pluginthat plugin's service, from your serverwhat the plugin sends; Mira tells you before
    Your party link through Tailscale FunnelTailscale's relays, then your guestsyour guests' traffic to Mira's page, encrypted to your PC. Your PC's Tailscale name becomes public
    A trailer from the web, at your clickthe trailer's site (often YouTube), in your browserwhat your browser sends (your IP address, the site's cookies). Mira loads nothing from it before your click and has no web view; a trailer kept on your server goes through it alone
    AltStore (iPhone)Apple, miracinema.app, GitHubyour Apple ID to Apple only, for the free certificate; Mira's source and app

    On your devices#

    • Your Jellyfin password is never stored: only the session. Windows protects it for your account; Mac and iPhone keep it in the keychain (on iPhone, this device only, never in backups).
    • Built-in Tailscale's keys and state are encrypted on the device. Its log stays there and holds no sign-in page.
    • Nothing about you or your library is ever collected.

    Full details, for each request: SECURITY.md (in French).

    Report a problem#

    Found a security issue? Use Security → Report a vulnerability on Mira's GitHub repository, never a public issue. Never post a token, a session file or a password anywhere public.
